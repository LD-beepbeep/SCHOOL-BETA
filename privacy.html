Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026 Version 2.0
Short version: Student OS stores your study data (notes, tasks, grades, flashcards) in your own Firebase account so it can sync across devices. We collect no advertising data, sell nothing to anyone, and you can delete everything at any time.

Section 2

Data We Store

When you are signed in, the following data is saved to your Firestore database document:

  • Notes (title, content, creation date, version history)
  • Tasks and task lists
  • Grade entries and course information
  • Flashcard decks and individual cards
  • Pomodoro session statistics
  • App preferences (theme, font, widget layout)
  • AI provider API keys you choose to save (Groq, Gemini) — stored in your Firestore document, not on any server we control

Guest / offline mode: If you use Student OS without signing in, all data is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device.

Section 3

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking scripts beyond Firebase's own infrastructure logging.
  • We do not read the content of your notes, tasks, or grades.
  • We do not store your AI API keys on any server we operate — they go directly to Firestore under your own account.
Section 4

Firebase as Data Processor

Student OS uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) to handle identity and cloud storage. Firebase acts as a data processor on our behalf. Your data is stored on Google's servers under the Firebase free Spark plan.

Google's privacy practices for Firebase are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Section 5

Authentication

You can sign in using:

  • Google — handled entirely by Firebase/Google OAuth.
  • GitHub — handled by Firebase's GitHub provider. We only receive your GitHub display name and email to create your account record.
  • Email and password — stored in Firebase Authentication, not in Firestore or any database we control directly.

We never see or store your passwords.

Section 6

Your Rights

  • Access: You can export all your data at any time using the Auto-Backup feature in Settings.
  • Correction: You can edit any note, task, grade, or flashcard directly in the app.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual items at any time. To delete your entire account and all associated data, use Settings › Account › Delete Account, or contact us directly.
  • Portability: The Auto-Backup export produces a standard JSON file you can keep or import into other tools.
Section 7

Children

Student OS is designed for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what Firebase Authentication requires for account creation. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete the data promptly.

Section 8

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and display a notice inside the app. Continuing to use Student OS after the notice constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
